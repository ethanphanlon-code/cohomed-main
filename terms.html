3. Documents

Documents are generated from professionally drafted templates, populated with the information your house enters. They are a starting point for professional review.

You must have your documents reviewed by a qualified Queensland solicitor before anyone signs them. Property co-ownership has serious, long-lasting legal consequences, and a template cannot account for your specific circumstances.

You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you enter. Documents generated from incorrect information will be incorrect.

4. Government schemes

Scheme information reflects criteria published by the relevant authority at the time of writing. Criteria, caps and availability change without notice. Any eligibility indication in the app is your own self-assessment against those published criteria — it is not a determination, and it is not a promise that you will receive anything. Always confirm against the official source before relying on it.
