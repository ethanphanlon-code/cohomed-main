The problem

You can afford a mortgage. Just not on your own.

Individually, most young Queenslanders can’t clear the deposit hurdle. Collectively, a share house often already has enough — the money is there, it’s just spread across four people with no structure holding it together.

The paperwork is the real barrier. Property law assumes couples and families. Buying with friends means agreements nobody has drafted before, contribution records nobody is keeping, and government schemes with eligibility rules that change depending on how many of you there are.

Brisbane’s median house is now $1.2 millionAlone, that is out of reach for most people. Between four, the deposit is around $60,000 each.

Renting

$2,400 a month between four people. After six years that’s $172,800 paid toward someone else’s mortgage, and you own nothing at the end of it.

Co-owning

The same $2,400 a month, going into an asset the four of you hold in shares you agreed in writing before anyone moved in.

Queensland schemes

The help you may already be entitled to.

There is real money available to first home buyers in Queensland, and the rules are not written with share houses in mind. CoHomed lays out each scheme’s published criteria against what your house has entered, so you can assess for yourselves where you stand.

Some schemes cap the number of applicants. A cap of two cannot cover a house of four — the app flags that rather than letting you find out late.

First Home Owner Grant

A grant toward a new-build first home under the published value cap. Administered by the Queensland Revenue Office.

First Home Concession

A transfer duty concession on a first home, assessed on price and the share each person holds.

Boost to Buy

The Queensland shared-equity scheme, with capped places in each round.

First Home Guarantee

A Commonwealth guarantee for low-deposit purchases, administered by Housing Australia through participating lenders.

Being clear

What CoHomed is, and what it isn’t.

Buying a home with other people is a serious financial decision. It matters that you know exactly what this app does for you and where you still need professional help.

What we do

  • Prepare documents from information you provide
  • Set out published scheme criteria so you can self-assess
  • Compile your own figures into a pack you can take anywhere
  • Keep the whole house looking at the same numbers

What we don’t

  • Give legal, financial or credit advice
  • Recommend a lender or broker, or take a referral fee
  • Handle money between you and anyone else
  • Decide whether you qualify for anything

Have your documents reviewed by a Queensland solicitor before signing, and confirm any scheme against its official source.
